THE ARNOLD SPLIT

Six days. Two muscle groups per day. Arnold's own signature move — antagonist supersets (chest paired with back, biceps paired with triceps) — is what let him run this much volume without the session running forever: rest one muscle while you work its opposite.

  • Day 1 & 4: Chest + Back — supersetted, antagonist-pair style
  • Day 2 & 5: Shoulders + Arms — biceps/triceps supersetted, delts standalone
  • Day 3 & 6: Legs — main lift pyramid + isolation, run straight (no superset)
  • Each muscle group trained twice per week — real training frequency, not just volume in one sitting
  • Every exercise's last set is taken to true failure — supersets keep the clock reasonable even at higher volume

Train Like The Oak
Six days. Every muscle hit twice. Supersets aren't optional here — they're the whole mechanism. Chest and back work together; biceps and triceps work together. Rest one side while you load the other.

Push every isolation exercise to the top of its rep range before adding weight. The last set of everything is a genuine failure set — that's the growth signal, not just showing up.

Legs get no shortcuts either — a real pyramid, then isolation, same as everything else.
