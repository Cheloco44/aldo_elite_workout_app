STRONGLIFTS 5×5

SQUAT · BENCH · DEADLIFT

Built around the big 3. Add weight every session. That's the whole program. Squat, bench, and deadlift headline the two workout days — overhead press and barbell row ride along to keep your shoulders and back balanced.

  • Squat & Bench Day: Squat 5×5 · Bench Press 5×5 · Overhead Press 5×5
  • Squat & Deadlift Day: Squat 5×5 · Deadlift 1×5 · Barbell Row 5×5
  • Alternate the two days — 3 sessions per week, rest day between each
  • Add 5 lbs per session on upper body, 10 lbs on squat and deadlift
  • Miss reps 3 sessions in a row on a lift? Deload that lift 10% — automatically
  • Fixed 10-week block: Weeks 1–8 build weight normally
  • Week 9: work up to a true 2-rep max on squat, bench & deadlift — get your estimated 1RM and powerlifting-meet attempt suggestions
  • Week 10: deload everything, then start your next block from a freshly tested max

Simple. Brutal. Effective.
Two workouts. Five lifts. Add weight every session. That's it — that's the whole program.

5×5 works because it forces you to get stronger on the movements that matter most. Don't overcomplicate it. Don't add extra work because you feel good. Trust the process and add the weight when the sets are done.

If a weight stalls, deload and come back. The bar will move again — it always does when you stay consistent.
