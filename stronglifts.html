STRONGLIFTS 5×5

CLASSIC 5×5

Two workouts. Five barbell lifts. Add weight every session. That's the whole program. Stronglifts works because it forces you to get stronger on the movements that matter — consistently, session after session.

  • Workout A: Squat 5×5 · Bench Press 5×5 · Barbell Row 5×5
  • Workout B: Squat 5×5 · Overhead Press 5×5 · Deadlift 1×5
  • Alternate A and B — 3 sessions per week, rest day between each
  • Add 5 lbs per session on upper body, 10 lbs on squat and deadlift
  • Miss reps 3 sessions in a row on a lift? Deload that lift 10%
